Who we are

More than a meeting. A community built around women supporting women.

Founded in October 2025, Grace Women Community began as a simple monthly gathering of women who wanted a stronger sense of connection, support and collective progress. In one year, that idea has grown into a vibrant network rooted in unity, service, empowerment and shared responsibility.

Our meetings create room for meaningful conversations, welfare support, community projects, mentorship, celebration and practical opportunities for members to grow.
